Model, update quality & limitations

The model pools related agents by generation, known parent network and search recipe. It fits wins, draws and losses jointly with a shared Black advantage and an opening effect shared by repeated games on the same start. All opening types are pooled. Shrinkage reduces noisy leader estimates; equal-sized subsets address the extra opportunity from having more candidates.

The strongest-existing-member estimate still benefits from a genuinely larger roster. The equal-tries column averages the best of a uniformly chosen subset of existing agents in each posterior draw; it does not predict newly invented agents. Registered inactive agents remain part of the comparison.

New game results update posterior weights against the last full fit, including complete affected opening blocks. Updates are accepted only with Pareto k below 0.7 and at least 400 effective weighted draws (and 10% of the original draws). Otherwise a full fit runs in the background. The last accepted result remains visible if a fit is pending or fails.

Credible intervals depend on this model and its priors. They do not establish transitive strength in every matchup or guarantee future performance. Historical validation and prior-sensitivity findings are saved in the original study; those historical tests are not rerun on every refresh.
